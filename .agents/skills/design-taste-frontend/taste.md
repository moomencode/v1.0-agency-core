# Taste: Design identity & taste

## Core principles
• Every UI decision must signal the brand's market position and audience
• Typography, color, motion, and density must work as a cohesive system, not isolated choices
• No generic "startup" or "template" feel — each element must feel intentional and owned
• The designer's taste level is the product's taste level

---

## Overall style direction
**Our design approach blends restrained sophistication with subtle warmth — a nod to heritage without being traditional, luxurious without being ostentatious.**

We prioritize clarity, quiet confidence, and the kind of elegance that comes from restraint, not ornament. Every component should feel like it belongs in a refined editorial spread or a well-curated boutique hotel.

---

## Brand personality
**Heritage-rooted, quietly confident, and unmistakably refined.**  
We are not loud, but we are seen. We are not trendy, but we are current. The brand speaks with the assurance of something that has always been there — timeless, grounded, and deeply intentional.

---

## Tone of voice in design
- Typography that feels established, not fashionable
- Color that whispers rather than shouts
- Motion that responds to intent, not attention-seeking
- Density that respects the user's focus

---

## Visual hierarchy
- **Typography leads hierarchy; color supports, never competes**  
  Weight, size, and letter-spacing do the work before a single color is applied
- **Every screen has one primary focal point** — secondary elements don't fight for attention
- **Generous whitespace is not empty; it is purposeful breathing room for premium feel**
- **Content priority drives layout density**, not the other way around

---

## Layout & spacing
- **80–100 character max-width for comfortable reading**
- **Vertical rhythm: 8px base unit, 24px preferred section gap, 40px for major shifts**
- **Content is centered, but not rigidly symmetrical** — asymmetric balance > mirrored layouts  
- **Multi-column layouts: 2-column > 3-column** — 3 equal columns feel templated
- **Cards never stretch full-width** — max-width with generous side padding

---

## Imagery & Iconography
- **Photography is editorial, not stock** — every image tells a story
- **Icons are minimal, semantic, never decorative** — if it doesn't communicate, remove it
- **Icon style: outlined, 1.5–2px stroke, rounded caps, consistent optical sizing**
- **No generic illustration or abstract 3D renders** — they age faster than photography

---

## Interactive & motion guidelines
- **Hover states are essential on every clickable element** (cards, buttons, links, icons)
- **Focus rings: 2–3px offset, not outline — use box-shadow for consistency**
- **Micro-interactions serve feedback, not decoration** — scale(0.97) on press, smooth transitions
- **Motion is utilitarian: 200–300ms for micro, 400–600ms for macro, 800ms max**
- **Easing: ease-in-out for fade, cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) for spring-like entrances**
- **Dark mode: luminance-aware transitions** — avoid jarring brightness shifts

---

## Typography system
- **Type scale: 6 steps from caption (0.75rem) to display (4.5rem+)**
- **Headings use a serif for editorial weight; body uses a clean sans-serif for readability**
- **Line-height: headings 1.1–1.2, body 1.6–1.8**
- **Letter-spacing: headings 0–0.02em, display 0.04–0.08em, body 0–0.01em**
- **Paragraph max-width: 65–70ch — never full-width**
- **Font loading: swap with preconnect, subset where possible**

---

## Color application
- **Primary: Deep, muted, sophisticated** — not pure primary colors (avoid #000 black, #fff white)
- **Accent: One restrained metallic or jewel tone used sparingly**
- **Surfaces: Warm off-whites (dark mode: warm charcoals, not pure #111)**
- **Text: High contrast but reduced harshness — e.g. #1a1a1a not #000, #e0e0e0 not #fff**
- **Gradients: Single-axis, subtle, under 30° angle — never multi-stop rainbows**

---

## Density & rhythm
- **Desktop: generous (padding 16–24px, section gaps 80–120px)**
- **Mobile: cozy but not cramped (padding 12–16px, section gaps 48–64px)**
- **Touch targets: minimum 44×44px, ideally 48×48px**
- **Forms: spacious inputs (min 48px height), clear labels, visible focus states**
