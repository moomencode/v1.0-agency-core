# Template: Client Proposal

> The shell Sales fills with scope, tier, timeline, deliverables, and
> terms. Client-facing; polished, honest, specific.

## Structure

```
1. The Opportunity        — one paragraph: who they are, what's missing
2. What We'll Build       — scope list (exactly what's in the Website doc)
3. Pricing                — tier name + band (exact amount by ops)
4. Timeline               — min–max days + milestones
5. Deliverables           — site, languages, screenshots, sitemap, release notes
6. Terms                  — payment, revisions, hosting, maintenance
7. Next Step              — one clear call to action
```

## Filling Rules

- Scope mirrors `website.buildParams` — never add sections not built.
- Every claim about the site is backed by the Review (`reviewId`) — the
  QA verdict is quoted as quality evidence.
- Facts (city, business name, type) come from the dossier, not memory.
- Exact price is an ops decision inside the tier band; the proposal
  states the band, not a number, unless overridden.
- Language adapts to the prospect's locale (via Sales input `language`).

## Outreach Draft (email/whatsapp)

- Subject ≤ 8 words, specific.
- Body ≤ 3 short paragraphs:
  1. Who we noticed them / why (specific fact),
  2. What we can deliver (1–2 lines),
  3. One next step ("can I send you a sample page? reply yes").
- No testimonials, no metrics, no fake urgency.

## Guardrails

Never fabricate capabilities, prices, deadlines, or success claims.
Never mention other clients by name.
